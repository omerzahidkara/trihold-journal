# Interested in collaborating?

I'm developing an independent low-poly science-fiction RTS under the temporary codename **Trihold**. The project has a technical prototype foundation and a growing design direction. It also has substantial unfinished work—in art, animation, effects, UI, testing and production quality.

I'm especially interested in connecting with **stylized 3D artists**, **animators**, **VFX and technical artists**, **RTS players/designers** and developers interested in multiplayer strategy.

This is an invitation to talk, **not a formal job listing or a promise of employment, funding or compensation**. Roles, schedules, asset rights, credit and any potential commercial arrangement would need to be discussed before committing to work.

If you reach out, tell me what interests you about the game and, if relevant, share examples of your work or the kinds of problems you enjoy solving.
