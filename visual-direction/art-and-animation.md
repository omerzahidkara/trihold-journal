# The visual direction is still being built

The intended visual identity is **stylized low-poly science fiction** with clear shapes, attractive color and readable top-down gameplay.

Strong silhouettes matter: infantry, Scout Cars, Mines and Outposts should be recognizable at a strategy camera distance. Their states should also be legible: movement, damage, exhaustion, construction, capture, wounded personnel and other combat information.

The final 3D art, animations, VFX, sound and cohesive presentation are **not finished**. Prototype assets currently serve implementation and testing. I would rather present that honestly than fill this repository with unrelated illustrations or misleading 'in-game' imagery.

I would especially welcome conversations with people interested in **low-poly 3D modeling, environment design, animation, VFX, technical art and strategy-game readability**.

[Five authentic Unity prototype gameplay recordings are now available](../media/README.md), showing construction, combat, rescue and capture. Some footage is accelerated or trimmed. The recordings document playable mechanics; the art, animations and VFX are still incomplete.
