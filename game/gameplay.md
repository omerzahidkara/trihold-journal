# Gameplay systems and their purpose

This is a player-facing explanation of the current design, not a private technical specification.

## Credits, Mines and territory

Credits finance production and structures. Mines generate dependable income and occupy fixed sites of differing levels. Eligible nearby infantry are needed to build; Mine construction requires two workers in the current design. Outposts expand the player's construction reach while providing defensive fire. They also create positions an opponent can contest.

## Recon and information

Infantry placed in Recon mode can move around the map, discover collectible field fragments, and receive Credits and Command Points. Recon movement includes avoiding acid hazards, but it does not guarantee survival against enemy forces. More Recon soldiers may offer more opportunities, at the expense of infrastructure, military defense and player attention.

**Recon being stronger than Mines with roughly eight to ten infantry is a hypothesis, not an established optimal opening.** Field reward availability and enemy action matter.

## Squads and readiness

A Sergeant can lead a squad of infantry. Running consumes stamina, which can affect firing performance. Infantry can suffer different injury states. Leadership and Sergeant traits introduce further differences between forces; some parts of the Sergeant/morale integration still require fuller live acceptance.

## Scout Cars

Scout Cars are the major mobile military asset of the current Base 1 stage. They fight infantry and other vehicles, transport squads and assist wounded evacuation. They are expensive and limited. Infantry alone are very poor counters, making Scout Cars central to early combat timing and macro/micro decisions.

## Outpost capture

An Outpost is a powerful defensive structure. At Base 1, destroying one directly is extremely expensive, so capture is generally the realistic offensive option. The capture core requires a Sergeant and two assigned healthy Recruits in the capture area. Defenders, contesting forces, combat and positioning affect progress. An attacking Scout Car can deliver a squad but risks being destroyed by defensive fire.

Taking an Outpost does not guarantee holding it. Reinforcements may eliminate the attackers and reverse the outcome.

## Wounds and logistics

Wounded soldiers can be transported by an eligible Scout Car, and Infirmary treatment/recovery systems exist. This creates a decision about rescuing personnel versus protecting an expensive transport. Comprehensive vehicle repair is planned, not finished.

## Map awareness

Fog of war, terrain, structure memory, radar and minimap-related systems help make information incomplete and valuable. A contact on the map is not the same as complete knowledge of the enemy army.

## Interface and multiplayer

The project has Main Menu, options, local profile-entry UI, private code host/join lobby, ready states, selection panels and gameplay HUD work, with EN/TR localization in supported flows. These are prototype features, not a public account service or evidence of completed online matchmaking.
