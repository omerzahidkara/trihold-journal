# What exists today?

**Development snapshot: 28 October 2026.** This page separates implemented code, scoped test observations and work still ahead.

| Area | Current development foundation | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Menus and multiplayer session** | Main Menu, options, profile-entry UI, host/join code, lobby, ready states, starting Credits presets | Not a verified account system, public matchmaking service or release-ready online experience |
| **Economy and territory** | Credits, Mine sites and income, construction labor, Outposts and building reach | Economy tuning and some progression/recycling details remain open |
| **Units and combat** | Infantry movement and combat, squads, stamina, Scout Car combat and six-seat transport | Balance is not established by broad outside playtesting |
| **Capture and recovery** | Outpost garrison and capture, injury states, wounded transport and Infirmary treatment | Scoped acceptance does not equal exhaustive integrated testing |
| **Information and map** | Fog of war, remembered structures, topology, hazardous terrain, radar and minimap-related systems | Presentation, integration and testing still needed |
| **Sergeant development** | Leadership and trait mechanics, morale/pressure-related work, presentation and validation | Full integrated acceptance and representative balancing remain unfinished |
| **Player interface** | Selection, action and status HUD, EN/TR supported UI flows | Final visual consistency and usability need further work |
| **Technology progression** | Base 1 functional foundation and plans for advancement | Base 2 content and late-game systems are not delivered |

## Evidence versus expectation

**Developer-observed:** scoped manual tests of medical, garrison and capture mechanics; some combat engagements where six tired attackers lost to three defenders with a trait-bearing Sergeant.

**Not established:** a globally optimal opening, a balanced match metagame, fully tested online security/reconnection, completed victory/exit experience or a game ready for strangers to download.

**Still planned:** wider Command Point use, additional specialist infantry and vehicles, complete vehicle repair systems, heavier siege/breakthrough capabilities, and more advanced technology tiers.

## Immediate priorities

Complete and stabilize the initial match loop, finish important UI/economic/progression details, address networking/performance issues, verify the end-of-match experience, and get honest feedback from outside 1v1 players. Visual art, animation, effects, sound and feedback require substantial development.

This journal deliberately avoids publishing private task briefs, internal formulas or source-code architecture. A large body of implementation work is not evidence that the product is finished.
