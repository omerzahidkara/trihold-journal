# Future direction

*A design roadmap, not a delivery promise or a release calendar.*

## First: make Base 1 a coherent match

The most important near-term outcome is not simply adding another feature. It is a complete, understandable, stable session that real people can play, evaluate and finish. Work remains on progression, gameplay feedback, economy edges, victory/exit flow, multiplayer reliability, performance and genuinely representative 1v1 testing.

## Next: Base 2 changes the military questions

The early Scout Car-centered battlefield is intended to develop into a wider combined-arms problem: specialist soldiers, additional vehicles, stronger counters to armor, more ways to challenge buildings, improved support capabilities and meaningful Command Point spending.

An IFV and additional medical/support roles are among the design ideas, but this page does not claim they are already playable.

## Later: heavier conflict

A powerful tank intended for late-game assaults on fortified bases is part of the vision. Broader drone and advanced-system ideas are also being considered. Their specific designs, viability and production scope may evolve considerably.

## Across every phase: art and readability

Low-poly science-fiction assets, animation, VFX, technical art and a cohesive UI are not finishing touches. Players need to see threats, unit condition and tactical consequences clearly. These areas remain major gaps and possible opportunities for collaboration.

No public release date, studio staffing claim, fixed monetization plan or final product title is being announced here.
