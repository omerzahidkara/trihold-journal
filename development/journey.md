# The development journey

## An old ambition

As a lifelong gamer and computer science graduate, I have long wanted to make an RTS. My earlier software experience, internships and a mobile-game experiment gave me some grounding, but a multiplayer strategy game still looked too large for a solo developer.

## Starting the experiment

Improvements in AI-assisted development changed what seemed technically possible to prototype. In 2026 I began exploring a stylized sci-fi RTS with a browser multiplayer target, one main spendable currency, map control and eventually richer tactical decisions.

## Foundations to interactions

The project moved through menus and multiplayer session flow, RTS controls, economy, Mines and Outposts, Recon and field rewards, Scout Cars and infantry combat, fog of war, medical recovery and developing squad leadership/morale systems.

The most interesting work has been watching these pieces create connected situations rather than merely collecting more features.

## October 2026: integration, evidence and presentation

There is real code and scoped manual verification. There are also incomplete flows, technical risks, unproven balance expectations and unfinished art. I am opening this public journal to show the ideas and process without publishing private implementation documents.

The next meaningful milestone is outside players completing and enjoying an understandable multiplayer match, then using their experiences to improve the game.
