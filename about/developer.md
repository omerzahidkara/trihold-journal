# The developer behind the codename

I'm **Ömer Zahid Kara**, a computer science graduate and independent game developer.

I have played games across many genres for much of my life. Strategy games in particular made me interested in the unpredictable situations that can emerge from a relatively small number of rules and meaningful choices. Building an RTS of my own became a long-running ambition.

Internships, software-development experience and an earlier mobile-game experiment gave me a better understanding of both the pleasure and difficulty of creating software. An RTS still seemed larger than a realistic solo undertaking—until newer AI-assisted development capabilities made an ambitious prototype feel technically approachable.

That was the reason to **begin**, not a reason to claim the difficult problems had been solved. Networking, visual clarity, game balance, content production, technical reliability and discovering whether people actually enjoy a match all require sustained work and independent judgment.

I am now developing a stylized sci-fi RTS under the temporary codename **Trihold**. My goal is to explore a game where territorial economy, reconnaissance, military timing, unit readiness and preserving forces affect each other.

This public repository is part game introduction and part developer portfolio. It is not a public copy of the private Unity/C# source repository. I want people to understand the design thinking, what is genuinely implemented and where help would make a difference.
